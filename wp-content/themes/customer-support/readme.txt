== Description ==

Customer Support Theme is a website design template for creating an online customer support and service website. It provides a structured layout for presenting support information, service details, contact options, and business updates. The theme includes customizable sections for displaying content, service categories, and important announcements. Its responsive design adapts the website layout to desktops, tablets, and mobile devices. The homepage can feature introductory content, service highlights, and links to relevant pages. Navigation menus help visitors access different sections of the website. The Customer Support theme supports customization of colors, typography, images, and other visual elements through available settings. Its layout can accommodate contact information, support resources, and frequently asked questions. Social media links and contact forms can also be included where supported by the theme or compatible plugins. Customer Support Theme provides a foundation for organizing customer service content in one website. It can be configured to match a brand’s visual identity while keeping essential information accessible. Additional functionality, such as ticket management, live chat, or knowledge bases, depends on the plugins and integrations used with the theme.

== Installation ==

1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance -> Themes and click the 'Add New' button.
2. Type in Customer Support in the search form and press the 'Enter' key on your keyboard.
3. Click on the 'Activate' button to use your new theme right away.
4. Navigate to Appearance > Customize in your admin panel and customize to taste.
